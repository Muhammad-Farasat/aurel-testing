# Visual principles

Color, contrast, imagery, density and ornament. Use the entry format below
for every preference. IDs are shared across all topic files, so take the next
free `P-` number from `MY-DESIGN-PREFERENCES.md` and the topic files.

## Entry format

```
### P-000 <short name>

- Strength: tentative | moderate | strong
- Preference: <what the owner wants, in one or two sentences>
- Why it works: <the design reason, not just the look>
- Applies when: <project types, contexts>
- Does not apply when: <exceptions, or conflicts with client needs>
- Evidence:
  - <YYYY-MM-DD> <project>: "<owner's words>"
  - Reference <REF-000>: <what in it shows this>
```
