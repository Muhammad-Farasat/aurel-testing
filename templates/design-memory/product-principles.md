# Product principles

Reusable product lessons and research methods from past projects. The
innovation strategist and the Orchestrator read this file before generating
or judging ideas.

## Confidentiality rule

This file is shared across all clients. Write only what would be safe for any
future client to read:

- No client names, products, numbers, research findings, quotes or plans.
- No competitor analysis from a client project. That stays in the project's
  `competitor-analysis.md`.
- Write the general lesson, not the case. "Booking flows that ask for
  account creation before showing price lose people" is fine. "Acme's
  checkout lost 30% at signup" isn't.

## Entry format

```
### PP-000 <short name>

- Kind: principle | research method | anti-pattern
- Lesson: <one or two sentences, general>
- Why it holds: <the mechanism, or the evidence type behind it>
- Applies when: <kinds of products or users>
- Doesn't apply when: <exceptions>
- Seen in: <number of projects, no names>, last <YYYY-MM-DD>
- Strength: tentative | moderate | strong
```

Strength follows the same rule as design preferences: `tentative` after one
project, `moderate` after two or once the owner confirms it, `strong` when
the owner makes it a rule.
